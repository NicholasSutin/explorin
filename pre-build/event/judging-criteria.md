# Judging Criteria

Source: judging-criteria slide shown at the event (OCR).

## Criteria

1. Creativity / Originality: unique and novel projects get a boost here.
2. Technical Complexity: is your hack technically impressive?
3. Adherence to Theme: judges are looking for hacks that lean into using AI.
4. Demo / Implementation: showing off what you built is just as important.

## Demo format

Judges will either come to your table or you will demo on stage, depending on the project count.
