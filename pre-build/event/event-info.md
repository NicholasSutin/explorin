# AI Engineer New York Hackathon (MLH)

## Key facts

- Date: Sunday, October 11, 2026
- Time: 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM (full schedule shared with attendees before the event)
- Location: 9th Ave and 31st St, Manhattan, New York, NY (exact address shown after registering)
- Google Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=40.752782478343136%2C-73.99874296493716
- Cost: free to participate
- Attendance: about 736 going (event full on Luma; waitlist open, applications still accepted)
- Presented by: Major League Hacking (MLH)
- Hosts: Major League Hacking, AI Engineer, Ryan Swift, Mary Siebert
- Host profiles: https://luma.com/user/mlhacks, https://luma.com/user/AIE, https://luma.com/user/thisisryanswift, https://luma.com/user/mpsiebert
- Bring your laptop; food, drinks, mentors, and community are provided

## About / what to expect

- Inaugural AI Engineer NYC Hackathon: a full day of building, experimenting, and meeting fellow AI engineers
- Explore new developer tools and get hands-on support from a team of mentors
- Come with or without a project idea, and with or without a team; there is a team formation activity and mentors to help brainstorm
- Time to build, experiment, and iterate on your project
- Opportunities to meet other AI engineers and share ideas
- Project demos and prizes
- Mentors to help you debug, brainstorm, and work through technical challenges
- Free food and drinks, including an evening happy hour

## Submission flow (mlh.com)

1. Register
2. Check In
3. Submit Project

All steps happen on the MLH event page: https://www.mlh.com/events/ai-engineer-nyc-2026

Every submitted project is judged for Best Overall (1st, 2nd, 3rd). Sponsor categories such as Sentry require opting in at submission time. See prizes.md and judging-criteria.md.
