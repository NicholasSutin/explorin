## About

Spend a day building at the inaugural AI Engineer NYC Hackathon!

​Join AI Engineer and Major League Hacking (MLH) on Sunday, October 11, for a full day of building, experimenting, and meeting fellow AI engineers.

Explore new developer tools, get hands-on support from our team of mentors, and perhaps build that side project you’ve been thinking about.

​You can come with or without a project idea, and with or without a team. We’ll have a team formation activity and plenty of mentors to help you brainstorm if you aren’t sure what you want to build.

​What to expect

- ​Time to build, experiment, and iterate on your project

- ​Opportunities to meet other AI engineers and share ideas

- ​Project demos and prizes

- ​Mentors to help you debug, brainstorm, and work through technical challenges

- ​Free food and drinks, including an evening happy hour

Best Use of Sentry (and Raffle)











Shipping an AI app in a weekend means things can break, and when an agent is involved it’s not always obvious why. Sentry shows you exactly what went wrong (errors, slow requests, and what your agent actually did) so you can fix it before demo time instead of during it.



## Ways to bring Sentry into your build this weekend



  - **Sentry MCP Server:** Connect Sentry to your coding agent and let it pull issues, stack traces, and root-cause context straight into your editor.
  - **AI Agent Monitoring:** Trace your agent’s LLM calls, tool calls, and token usage so you can see where it slows down or goes off the rails.
  - **Error Monitoring & Tracing:** Drop in the SDK and catch crashes and performance bottlenecks across your frontend and backend.
  - **Seer:** Let Sentry’s AI debugger find the root cause of an issue and suggest a fix.

Sign up for Sentry’s free 14-day trial (no credit card required) and check out the Sentry Cookbook for quick recipes to get started.
