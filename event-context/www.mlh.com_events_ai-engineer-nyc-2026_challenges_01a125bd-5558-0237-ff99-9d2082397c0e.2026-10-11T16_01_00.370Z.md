# Best Use of Sentry (and Raffle)

Event prize

Shipping an AI app in a weekend means things can break, and when an agent is involved it’s not always obvious why. Sentry shows you exactly what went wrong (errors, slow requests, and what your agent actually did) so you can fix it before demo time instead of during it.

## Ways to bring Sentry into your build this weekend

- **Sentry MCP Server:** Connect Sentry to your coding agent and let it pull issues, stack traces, and root-cause context straight into your editor.
- **AI Agent Monitoring:** Trace your agent’s LLM calls, tool calls, and token usage so you can see where it slows down or goes off the rails.
- **Error Monitoring & Tracing:** Drop in the SDK and catch crashes and performance bottlenecks across your frontend and backend.
- **Seer:** Let Sentry’s AI debugger find the root cause of an issue and suggest a fix.
