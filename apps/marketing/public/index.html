How it works

Three steps. One good day.

  1. Say what you're craving

    "A rainy afternoon with art and really good coffee." Explorin searches the web, picks real, named places, checks each one on the map, and shows how people like you felt there.

  2. Live your story

    Start the adventure. Jot a thought or say it out loud. Add a few photos along the way.

  3. Keep the feeling

    Your notes and photos become a scrapbook you can download or share. Photos stay on your device.
